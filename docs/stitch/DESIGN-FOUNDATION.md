# Woodhouse

Woodhouse is the public journal, observatory and portfolio of the Loftwah Software Factory. It is
not a SaaS dashboard and not a generated portfolio template. It is an engineering publication:
confident, dark, specific and dated. The site is dark on every device by design. There is no light
mode, no theme switch and no light surface between navigations.

## Visual authority

- Ink `#080D19` is the page ground. Raised surfaces stay in the same dark family: `#111A2C`,
  `#17233A`, `#070B13`. Use open layout and horizontal rules before adding another box.
- Acid lime `#E9FF49` marks human direction, the Woodhouse identity and one deliberate primary
  action. Do not flood a page with lime.
- Cyan `#79D8FF` marks real system paths and links. Cobalt `#647DFF` marks connected products.
  Coral `#FF746A` marks stops, failures and evidence boundaries. Colour never carries a state
  without a text label.
- Bricolage Grotesque carries display titles with deliberate line breaks, tight tracking and a
  confident left edge. Body copy is a humanist sans at a 65–75 character measure. Monospace is
  reserved for identifiers, dates, source labels and compact technical detail.
- Sentence case carries prose. Uppercase is for short labels only, never whole paragraphs.
- Crisp edges. No glassmorphism, decorative gradients, glowing AI imagery, oversized rounded
  cards or card-grid soup. Corners are 0–4px.

## Composition

- Lead with useful content, not an empty hero. The first viewport answers: what is this, who runs
  it, and what can I do right now? A large left-aligned headline, one plain sentence and one
  primary action.
- Use asymmetry deliberately: a dominant reading column with a narrow evidence rail, or a wide
  plate beside a shorter caption block. Do not centre every section.
- Horizontal rules separate editorial movements. Sections have generous vertical space
  (64–128px). Dense workbenches may be tighter, but remain legible.
- Every page has an obvious route back to the work: Projects, Stories, Analytics, Leaderboards,
  Share. The desktop header is one line with a search control and a secondary menu. On phones the
  header reduces to WOODHOUSE + MENU, and the menu is a full-width index.

## Product identity

Each project owns its artwork and colour. Product artwork comes from that product's published
Open Graph image and keeps its 1200×630 (or declared) aspect ratio. Woodhouse campaign artwork is
optional gallery material and is never presented as the product's official artwork. Credit product
artwork to its project and Woodhouse illustrations to Woodhouse.

## Screen families and their character

1. **Home** — editorial cover. Signature fibre plate, plain introduction, one primary action,
   then a dated portfolio readout, a compact project register and recent stories.
2. **Projects** — a ledger, not a card grid. Open rows separated by rules: artwork, name,
   discipline, plain hook, dated state, two actions (product action + "See how it works").
3. **Project record** — dossier. Large product artwork, what it is, why it matters, where the work
   stands, evidence, related story, honest proof boundary, then related work.
4. **Editorial** — long-form reading. A wide title block, a narrow measure for prose, a side rail
   for review date, evidence state and proof boundary. Pull quotes, technical plates, image plates
   and evidence links are full editorial citizens.
5. **Analytics** — an analytical research workbench inside the same world. Narrow project rail,
   compact heading and date toolbar, one dominant chart, interpretation beside it. Never add
   measures with different units into a cross-product total. Missing data stays a gap; reported
   zero stays zero; coverage and quality labels are always visible.
6. **Share** — an image-first gallery. Campaign artwork and real product captures lead. Project
   and media-type filters, six previews per page, native copy/download beside each asset. No
   reading prerequisite and no setup step.
7. **Exhibits** — bounded interactive islands with their own authored visual languages: the patch
   bench (machined, 3D, lit) and the source plate (flat, printed, registered). Controls, status and
   explanation live outside the canvas. Reduced motion keeps every function.
8. **Factory / supporting** — working connections, architecture, Agent Reception, operator
   biography, contact, doctrine. Technical but plain-spoken.
9. **States** — 404, unavailable, empty, loading, failed copy/download, missing artwork. Every
   state names what happened, keeps the person's input where possible and offers a next step.
   Never show a blank panel where a section should be.

## Interaction

- Every control has a visible keyboard focus ring in acid lime. Touch targets are at least 44px.
- Nothing depends on hover.
- A changed control acknowledges input immediately (selected state, pending label) and names the
  work while it runs. Failures say what failed and how to recover.
- Motion is small and purposeful, except the homepage signature plate and the two working
  exhibits. Their motion must be interruptible, and reduced motion keeps the whole composition.
- Source artwork links open the product. Woodhouse titles open the Woodhouse record. Keep that
  distinction visible in the link wording.

## Responsive

- Phone reading order is authored, not merely stacked. The homepage names the page with its `h1`
  before the decorative fibre plate.
- Phones: single column, full-bleed plates, horizontal project rails and filter rows with a
  visible scroll affordance, no horizontal page overflow.
- Tablet: two-column editorial blocks; charts and galleries gain width but keep one measure.
- Desktop: 1440px max content width, 72px side gutters, wide plates that use the available width.
- Large desktop: increase whitespace and plate size, not font size indefinitely.

## Evidence and honesty

- Every claim has a source and a review date.
- Implementation, qualification, deployment and physical use are distinct states.
- Do not invent analytics, testimonials, reader numbers, deployment results or capabilities.
- Do not combine measurements with different units into a total.
- A demonstration is not adoption; a copy is not a licence; a simulation says it is one.

## Copy

Warm, direct Australian English. Concrete verbs and contractions. Explain what someone can do and
why it is interesting. Keep internal operator vocabulary ("dossier", "qualification", "admitted
project") out of introductory copy. British/Australian spelling: colour, behaviour, organisation,
recognise. Dates read as "6 November 2026"; technical dates remain ISO 8601 where they identify a
record.
