# Home and project artwork

- **Scope and mode:** Home page, with the project index and project dossier as connected reading surfaces. Experience: visitors explore the work itself.
- **Audience and job:** A curious builder or collaborator should understand the factory quickly, see the actual products, and follow a project to its current reviewed record.
- **Proof and content:** The home field shows current project-site artwork with its source title, description, favicon and link. The complete record contains eight projects, one review date, a real factory flow diagram and clear evidence boundaries.
- **Constraints:** Keep the system dark regardless of OS preference. Load source imagery directly from each project's current site metadata; retain its 1200×630 framing, link and attribution. Use a clearly labelled project-specific Woodhouse SVG only for a project without a public site or when its source image fails. Never invent project activity or imply promotional art proves product quality. Keep source details in natural visitor language, not technical publishing jargon.
- **Chosen direction:** A dark, web-native engineering journal, with an eight-project D2 network as the first-view focal point, then the source sites' own artwork and their full project record. A separate factory-flow diagram explains human review, qualification, production and evidence without implying that every project has passed those gates.
- **Composition reference:** Exploratory generated studies helped compare energy and image/map hierarchy. Their invented project names, people, dates and activity are rejected; they are not content or asset sources.
- **Unresolved decisions:** None for this pass. Woodhouse remains a fixed dark-mode publication with no theme toggle.
