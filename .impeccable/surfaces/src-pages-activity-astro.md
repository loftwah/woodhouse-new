# Portfolio activity

Reviewed 15 October 2026. Mode: Operate / Read. This brief describes the built surface and preview evidence; it does not establish production deployment or physical-device acceptance.

## Scope and direction

The observed failure was an activity hub that could explain radio listening but could not show whether the wider products were used. The #34 mission extends `/activity/` to Woodhouse, App Composer, Fighter, SHOALSHOT, Protocol 11, LoftwahFM and Bubbles. Visitors choose a reporting window or project, understand real measurements and their coverage, then download a report image or take its permanent link. Products keep their gameplay, saves, identities, competitive verification and release gates. Public membership remains the existing published, public-safe CMS boundary, with Woodhouse itself; this surface creates no parallel identity catalogue.

The visual world remains the Woodhouse technical publication: ink ground (`#080D19`), acid lime (`#E9FF49`), locally hosted Bricolage Grotesque, strong type and decisive rules. The reading task becomes more operational without introducing a second design system. Measurements earn their space through source data; no decorative analytics wallpaper, gradients or card soup. Global `DESIGN.md` remains the authority for shared identity, focus and reduced-motion treatment.

## Built composition

Source reviewed 15 October 2026: “The work. / The response.” leads the portfolio view, with available source figures in the opening composition rather than introductory prose alone. A selected project uses its own name. Native 7/30/90-day links preserve project selection, show the active period and sit beside the UTC report date and reporting coverage. The current day is explicitly partial. Links retain the separate Games × Radio report and share desk.

Sharing follows the opening controls; the project ledger follows sharing. Projects with supplied measurements lead the page without implying a popularity ranking. Open rows separated by horizontal rules put identity and credited source artwork alongside measurements. Project-title links open the project's activity view; artwork links open its own site. Source artwork retains its landscape aspect ratio. The existing fallback changes image text and visible credit to identify a Woodhouse illustration when source artwork fails.

Each row exposes up to six relevant metric fields in a definition list, with tabular figures and a text quality label. Browser returns appear when a source supplies them. At desktop widths the metrics use four columns; at narrower widths they use two. On phones the header and sharing areas stack, project identity precedes measurements, and controls wrap into the same reading order. The ledger remains substantive on phones.

Daily history uses native disclosure: lime bars provide a quick visual reading, while a captioned semantic table with scoped date and metric headers supplies the values. A separate native disclosure holds source links, source update time, limitations and metric definitions. Neither source detail nor daily detail requires a client framework.

## Measurement and missing states

Source reviewed 15 October 2026: exact received events, bounded counts, sampled estimates and unavailable measurements remain distinct. Visible wording includes Reported, Bounded count, Estimated and Not measured; missing values never become zero. A disconnected source receives an explicit awaiting-source state. Collection start, requested UTC period, stale-source wording and unknown coverage remain visible beside each project's figures.

Reported browser activity does not establish verified people, competitive scores, retention or unique returning audiences. The tab-session collector cannot identify returning browsers; a project-owned source may supply its own defensible browser measures. Games, app actions and radio listening retain separate meanings. Offline use, privacy choices and blocked requests can reduce collection. Complete-week records require exact daily history and comparable complete Monday–Sunday UTC periods; short, sampled, capped or partial history earns no record badge.

`/activity/measurement/` explains collection, exclusions, retention and the Woodhouse browser opt-out. It links back to reports and remains discoverable in the site footer. Project-owned privacy choices stay with the respective project. Header navigation and the phone index expose portfolio activity through the existing site shell.

## Saved reports and sharing

Source reviewed 15 October 2026: sanitized aggregate report snapshots are saved in the existing D1 binding. A report permalink freezes its figures and reporting window; its page, caption, public JSON and generated PNGs use that saved report. Current public membership is rechecked. A saved report identifies itself and offers a latest-report link, with older-report wording when applicable. A missing saved report is unavailable rather than silently replaced with today's figures.

Download image is the lime primary action and uses a native attachment link. Copy caption + link writes the report's caption and permalink, with a visible status message and link fallback on failure. Share image progressively appears after the PNG is prepared and file sharing is supported. Cancellation is silent; other share failure directs the reader to download. Controls and disclosure summaries retain a minimum 44px target height.

The native preview disclosure offers three authored PNG compositions: landscape (1200 × 630), square (1200 × 1200) and portrait (1080 × 1920). These layouts use the same source figures and coverage, composed for each shape rather than cropped. They carry Woodhouse identity, reporting dates, labelled limitations and explicit awaiting-measurement rows. Do not mistake a generated export for a published social post or human sharing acceptance.
