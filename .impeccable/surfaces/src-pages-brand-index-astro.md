# Surface brief — /brand/ (house identity)

Reviewed 11 November 2026.

**Mode:** Read, with one Experience moment. The visitor leaves knowing what the house face
is, why it exists and how to use it. The film is the moment; everything around it is
reference material that has to stay findable.

**Primary target:** `src/pages/brand/index.astro`
**Related:** `scripts/brand/loom-font.py`, `scripts/brand/render-film.mjs`,
`public/brand/*`, `public/fonts/loom-display.*`, `src/components/SiteFooter.astro`

## What this surface must do

1. Name the typeface, show it set correctly, and say plainly where it stops (no lowercase,
   no ampersand, no at-sign).
2. Make the drawing inspectable: the four decisions, and construction plates built from the
   font's own outlines rather than a redraw.
3. Carry the rest of the kit that already governed the site — seal, palette with measured
   contrast, the two-display-face split, motion laws, voice rules.
4. Hand over real files, not screenshots of files.
5. Say what is decided and what is not. No claim that a passing build is a reader's acceptance
   or MP's taste acceptance.

## Committed visual world

Ink ground, acid for the house name, cyan for system paths, rules instead of boxes, display type
at chapter scale. Loom sets the name; Bricolage sets the argument; the system sans reads; mono
carries identifiers and measurements.

## Deliberate non-goals

- Not a restyle of existing pages, and not a third display face.
- Not a logo-policy document. The seal's rules are practical (clear space, minimum size, two
  treatments), not enforcement.
