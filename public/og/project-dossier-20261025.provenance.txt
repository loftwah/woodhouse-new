Current file: Woodhouse's own project-dossier social card, drawn 25 October 2026. 1200 x 630 JPEG, 118,116 bytes.

This is not a generated illustration and not product artwork. The material is the signature the homepage already draws: a lens of tensioned fibres, twisted and lit once. It was captured from the live canvas at `/#signature` on 25 October 2026 by reshaping the signature stage to a wide crop and screenshotting only the canvas — no page chrome, no model, no third-party image service. The composition over it is typeset from the repository's own Bricolage Grotesque TTFs at real 1200 x 630 dimensions.

Retained source: `scripts/assets/dossier/loom-master.png` (1600 x 1000 capture, exposure 2 of 3). Composition source: `scripts/render-dossier-preview.mjs`, which reads that master and writes this card. Both are checked in so the card can be re-derived exactly rather than re-guessed.

Why it carries no count and no date. The card it replaces said "ONE HUMAN · EIGHT PROJECTS" over "REVIEWED 28 SEPTEMBER 2026". Both claims were true when they were drawn and both were wrong by October: the portfolio had ten published projects, and the review date named one document rather than the thing being shared. A social card outlives the page it was drawn for — it stays in other people's posts and cached unfurls for years. So this card makes no claim that can rot. It says what a dossier is, which does not change.

What it does not claim. It is not an image of any product, and it is not evidence that anything shipped. The fibres are drawn, not photographed; the light is composed, not measured. Where a project publishes its own artwork, that artwork is used instead and this card is not shown.

Cropping. Designed for 1.91:1, which is what X, Facebook, LinkedIn, Slack and iMessage ask for. It reads at 300 px wide, where only the headline survives. A centre-square crop takes part of the headline with it; the artwork alone still reads as a finished frame. No square variant is claimed.
