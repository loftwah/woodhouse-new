WOODHOUSE SOCIAL ART
13 November 2026, Melbourne

Every piece in this series comes directly from Codex ImageGen, using the actual project imagery as reference. The type and addresses are drawn by the model where they appear. No marks, lettering, URLs, screenshots or images have been layered onto these compositions. The original PNG is included. JPEG exports use only resizing and encoding.

Night Signal references the published LoftwahFM station art. Bank the Shot references the Bubbles arcade drawing. Links and a Guestbook references the public Creator Hub study. The two Moss illustrations reference the public Foilbury character. Dive the Tide Pool references the Glimmerfin study. The Discord banners reference the published Woodhouse workshop and Bubbles arcade imagery, and leave the server name area quiet. Viking vs Reaper references the Fighter storyboard.
