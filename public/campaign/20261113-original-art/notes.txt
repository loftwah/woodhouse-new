WOODHOUSE ORIGINAL ART
13 November 2026, Melbourne

The ten process drawings are the original PNGs produced with Codex ImageGen for Making the Work. The 30 earlier campaign illustrations are lossless WebP versions of the images used in Product Social and Field Notes. This gallery presents them before the campaign titles, official marks and links were applied. Some illustrations interpret shapes or icons from the product reference.

The drawings were made for the retrospective and are labelled accordingly in the published campaign. For the finished poster, capture, exact product links and social sizes, go to https://woodhouse.loftwah.com/share/.
