PROVENANCE
Workers AI Lucid Origin produced the original 1280 × 720 still. The selected
refinement was a real masked fill: a white ellipse in the empty upper-right panel,
then FLUX.1 Fill returning a 1280 × 704 variant, with the original, mask and
result kept separate. Hailuo 2.3 Standard animated the selected edit and a
separate Studio Effect supplied the sound bed. The exact type, approved App
Composer mark, crops and the 9:16 frame were finished deterministically, because
generated lettering and a generated logo would be the wrong tool. Hashes and
lineage: https://composer.loftwah.com/media-studio#made

The artwork is generated promotional material with the product's exact type and

images, so this placement shows the card and offers the portrait, square and
