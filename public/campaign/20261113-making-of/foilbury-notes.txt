# Foilbury

A. The lantern grove keeper.
B. Moss, in holographic.
Caption. Moss keeps the lamps lit. Turn the card in Foilbury’s public cabinet and see what the finish does at https://foilbury.loftwah.com/cabinet/moss.

A is Codex ImageGen editorial character art based on the public Moss card, with the original hat, teal hoodie, leaf charm and face. B is a generated brass-and-plum cabinet scene to frame the actual whole card. The card used for the latter is the native public holographic render, credited to Foilbury. The gallery states the underlying Moss artwork origin as unknown. The use here is the owner's requested promotion of a first-party published product render, with no standalone relicensing or collectible release claims.
