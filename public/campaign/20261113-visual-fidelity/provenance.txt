Woodhouse visual fidelity studies
Reviewed 13 November 2026, Melbourne. Three editorial works made with built-in Codex ImageGen.
The PNGs are the unedited outputs. The JPEGs use proportional framing, resizing and encoding.
No paid image API was called for this batch.
